I kept spinning up little sites — a mood score for the world, a daily
songbird, a browser groovebox, a whole lab of generative odds and ends —
and they were scattered across a handful of repos with no front door.

This is the front door. Nothing fancy: a short intro, links to the things
I've built, and the occasional note here when I make something new or learn
something worth writing down.

Most of these were built alongside Claude, one idea at a time. More soon.
